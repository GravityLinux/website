Gravity Linux is a project to bring Linux to modern Apple Silicon Macs (M4 and
above) and to upstream that support into the Linux kernel and all the
userspace projects it depends on. Everything in it is built by clean-room
reverse engineering: we do not look at Apple source code, disassembly, or
leaked technical information, our methods and procedures are public, and we
publish artifacts sufficient to verify the clean-room nature of our work. If we
do this right, Gravity Linux the distro eventually goes away, because the
support it carries will simply be part of every other Linux distribution.

We're looking to put out our first release very soon.
